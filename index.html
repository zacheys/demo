The Farm

Historic charm meets modern comfort.

Meadow's End Farm is a restored 1834 farmhouse nestled at the end of a quiet dirt road in Chester, Vermont. With wide plank floors, exposed ceiling beams, original 12-over-12 windows, and an open, airy feel, this home carries nearly two centuries of character while offering every modern convenience.

Surrounded by meadows, forest, and a small pond on 11 private acres, this is the kind of place where you slow down, breathe deep, and remember what a real getaway feels like.
